Block Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Block Puzzle (the "App"), developed by Bobomurod Mamatqobilov (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS.

In short: we do not collect, store or sell any of your personal information ourselves. The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your device, so your progress is kept between sessions:

  • game progress: completed levels, stars, best score and coin balance;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration and language.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App. The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads: banners in the menus, an occasional full-screen ad between rounds, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for coins or to continue a game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS see below);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps).

Google acts under its own privacy policy. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and data protection

3. Your choices about ads

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the App, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the App.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • iOS: the App does not ask for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with advertisers. You can also control tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Choosing non-personalized ads does not remove ads; it only means the ads you see are not based on your interests.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

5. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our servers. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google using the links above. You can still contact us with any question and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
